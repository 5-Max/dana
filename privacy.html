Privacy Policy

Shears A Go Go  ·  Last updated: June 5, 2026

What information we collect

We collect your name, phone number, address, and appointment details. This information is provided directly to Dana during the booking process.

How we use your information

Your information is used solely to manage your appointments and send you SMS reminders and confirmations from Shears A Go Go. You may receive:

  • Day-before appointment confirmations
  • Departure reminders
  • Appointment updates from Dana

How to opt out

Reply STOP to any text message to stop receiving SMS notifications. You may also contact Dana directly to be removed from the system.

Data sharing

We do not sell, share, or distribute your personal information to any third parties.

Data storage

Your information is stored securely using Google Firebase. Only Dana and authorized app administrators have access to your data.

Contact

For questions or to request data removal, contact Dana directly.
